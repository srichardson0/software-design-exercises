EVERMORE ▢ THE MIRROR ROOM quiet

you are inside the store

EVERMORE is not a website. it is a small piece of the larger feeling you have already been carrying around: things are asking to be bought, that wanting is the same as needing, that the cart is a place where you live.

on the floor, products accumulate because you are there. you do not click them. you do not request them. they simply arrive, the way a banner ad arrives, the way an email arrives, the way a friend's haul-video arrives. the strobing ADD TO CART buttons do not exist to be clicked. they exist to make you tired.

at the counter, every item you brushed past is read back to you with a price attached. there is no refund. there is no undo. but the store remains open, and the doors remain unlocked, and you can go back in whenever you would like to.

that is the trick of it.
